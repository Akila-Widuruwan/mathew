A little about Mathew

A voice with purpose.
A story with heart.

Mathew Bandara is a Sri Lankan announcer, public speaker, voice-over artist and scriptwriter—bringing performance and language together to make messages matter.

From guiding a room through a live event to shaping the words behind a campaign, his work starts with listening. Understand the audience. Find the heart of the message. Then deliver it with clarity, warmth and feeling.
